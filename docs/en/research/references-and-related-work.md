# References and Related Work

Mind Gym draws from two broad streams: cognitive training patterns such as N-back and recall drills, and web engineering practices for resilient offline applications. The combination matters because the product must be usable in short, interruption-prone sessions without adding operational complexity.

This page is intentionally lightweight, but it marks the place where future citations, design references, and research summaries can accumulate without breaking the current site structure.
